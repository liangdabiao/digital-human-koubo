| Apple Vision | macOS system framework | Apple SDK | Person segmentation for the text-behind effect (`scripts/personmask.swift`, compiled locally on first use; macOS only). |
| Sound effects & placeholder music | generated by `scripts/make_assets.py` | MIT (this repo) | Synthesized from code — no samples. No third-party audio is shipped. |

Services you connect with your own account / 需要你自己账号的服务:
- [即梦 Dreamina](https://jimeng.jianying.com) — image & video generation (Seedance family, Jimeng image models) through the [official dreamina CLI](https://jimeng.jianying.com/cli). Usage is billed to your own Jimeng credits; content compliance follows the Jimeng/Dreamina terms of service.
- [apiz.ai](https://apiz.ai) — optional: speech-text alignment (script verification / word-level timing), Minimax TTS, voice cloning, backup generation channels. Uses your own API key.
- [edge-tts](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts) (pip package) — optional narration placeholder voices via the Microsoft Edge online TTS service. Follow the service's terms; don't abuse it.
- [Xiaomi MiMo](https://platform.xiaomimimo.com/) — optional TTS (voice design / voice cloning). Clone only voices you own or are licensed to use.

Research reports in `skills/digital-human-koubo/references/research/` describe editing techniques observed in publicly available videos, with timestamps for reference. They contain no copied footage, and quotes are kept under 15 words.
